Research

My main interests are virtual element methods and their implementation in high-performance computing, alongside numerical PDEs and reproducible scientific computing.

Virtual Element Methods (VEM)

Numerical methods on polygonal and polyhedral meshes, and PDE discretization on complex domains.

  • Discrete spaces and degrees of freedom
  • Projection operators and stabilization
  • Error analysis and numerical validation

High-Performance Computing (HPC)

Parallel assembly, sparse linear system solvers, and performance evaluation in scientific software.

  • Parallel matrix assembly
  • Sparse linear solvers
  • Scalability and performance testing

Scientific Computing

Numerical algorithms, computational experiments, visualization, and reproducible research workflows.

  • Numerical PDEs
  • Convergence studies
  • Documenting and reproducing computational results
